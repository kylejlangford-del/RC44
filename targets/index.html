Downwind & upwind targets

Optimum boat speed, angle and heel by true wind speed, built from Njord's raw telemetry for both boats across the season so far. Each point is a true 10-second segment — filtered to a steady point of sail and to at least 70% of target boat speed — not the 30-second summary Njord's own reports use.
